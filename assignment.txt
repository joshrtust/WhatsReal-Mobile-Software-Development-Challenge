# WhatsReal Mobile — Software Development Challenge

## Objective

You have been provided with a database containing information about WhatsReal Mobile customers and their associated mobile phone plans.

Your challenge is to design and develop a single-page application (SPA) that provides an intuitive way to access and explore this information.

Minimum Requirements

Your application must:

1. Display a list of users contained within the database.
2. Provide the ability to view the details of an individual user.
3. Use Angular as the frontend application framework.

Development Guidelines

You are free to select any additional technologies, libraries, tools and architectural approaches you consider appropriate for developing your solution. However, we 
ask that you refrain from using AI-assisted tools during this challenge.

As you explore the database, you may discover opportunities to introduce additional functionality, improve the user experience or provide greater value to the end user.

We encourage you to be creative, exercise your technical judgment and explore these opportunities while effectively managing your time.

Time Allocation

You have one business day from the time you receive this assignment to complete and submit your solution.

We are interested not only in what you build, but also in how you approach the challenge, the decisions you make and how you prioritize your work within the time available.

Good luck, and enjoy the challenge!
